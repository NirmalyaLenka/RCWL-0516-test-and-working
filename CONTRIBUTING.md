# Contributing

Contributions that improve accuracy, reproducibility or clarity are welcome.

- Cite primary documentation or clearly label observations and hypotheses.
- Do not invent an inventor, manufacturer, frequency tolerance or guaranteed detection range.
- Include board revision, supply voltage and test conditions for measurements.
- Keep Arduino and ESP32 examples simple and independently compilable.
- Do not copy third-party schematics, photographs or documentation without checking their licensing and attribution requirements.
- Report false-trigger behavior and installation details when opening an issue.

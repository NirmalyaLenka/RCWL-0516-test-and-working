# History, Origin and Attribution

## Who invented the RCWL-0516?

**The original inventor or design team of the specific RCWL-0516 board is not reliably identified in the public sources referenced by this repository.** Do not attribute it to a named person without primary evidence.

The module is widely sold by multiple vendors and is documented by community reverse-engineering work. Its signal-processing chip is commonly marked `RCWL-9196`, but the cited sources do not establish a transparent manufacturer history or a complete official datasheet for that chip.

## What can be established

- The RCWL-0516 is a commercially distributed microwave Doppler motion-sensor module.
- A frequently referenced research repository by **Joe Desbonnet** documents observations, circuit analysis, pin behavior, and links to schematic material. That makes Desbonnet a documented reverse-engineering contributor, **not necessarily the inventor of the module**.
- **John Taylor** contributed a clearer schematic referenced by the research repository. A schematic contribution does not establish authorship of the original product.
- The research repository discusses earlier patents and related microwave antenna / proximity-switch technology. Related patents show prior or related technology, but do not by themselves prove who designed or manufactured the RCWL-0516.
- Vendor datasheets describe typical operation and specifications, but the public documents reviewed do not establish a named individual inventor.

## How to research this further responsibly

1. Check the PCB markings and packaging of the specific module in hand.
2. Search for manufacturer identity, original product documentation and dated design records.
3. Read the cited patents' inventor, applicant and priority information; distinguish those inventions from the exact RCWL-0516 product.
4. Check the issue history and references in the community research repository.
5. Keep the wording “original inventor not verified” until primary evidence supports a stronger claim.
